# Gyms of War: No Pain, No Cancel — story and humor plan

Replaces the "Customer Retention" mall story (spec sections 2 and 4, the cast, the enemy and weapon names, and the barks). Mechanics, level sizes, encounter shapes and the build order stay as they are. Only the fiction, names and set dressing change.

## Ground rules

- **Every enemy is an alien.** No human is ever an enemy, and nothing on screen looks like a real workplace (no offices, cubicles or call centers). The only humans besides Doc and Tobble are trapped members you free.
- **Goo, not blood.** Aliens burst into acid-green gel, sweatbands and crumpled receipts.
- **The target of the satire is gym cancellation, not people.** That means auto-renew, "in person only", cancellation fees, upsells and the guilt of the January resolution. The aliens are the gym.
- **No real gym brands, slogans or trademarks** (for example, no "Judgement Free Zone" and no "lunk alarm"). Everything is GlorpFit.

## Premise

Seven years ago, on January 2nd, Doc Vex signed up for **GlorpFit Orbital**, a mega-gym on a space station. He went twice. He has been billed $49.99 every month since.

GlorpFit was quietly taken over by the **Glorp**, aliens that feed on unused memberships. To them, a member who never shows up is a crop, and guilt is the harvest. Their terms of service say cancellation must be done **in person, at the Cancellation Desk, on the top floor, during staffed hours (Tuesdays, 2:00–2:15 p.m.)**.

It is Tuesday. It is 1:30. Doc docks his shuttle, loads The Regret, and goes in.

**Tagline:** *"Cancellation must be made in person."*

## Cast

- **Doc Vex:** a dry, exhausted scientist who has read the terms of service and is very calm about what happens next. Lines are short and deadpan.
  - "I would like to cancel my membership."
  - "Is this the cancellation desk?" (it never is)
  - "I've been doing cardio. It's called running from your staff."
- **Tobble:** Doc's relentlessly upbeat intern, who loves the gym and wears a fitness tracker. He revives Doc by **spotting** him ("I got you! Push! PUSH! Two more!"). He keeps getting tempted by the deals ("Wait, six months free is actually good…"), and he logs every fight as a workout.
  - When a fight ends: "Ooh, you closed your rings!"
  - On a headshot: "That's a PR!"
- **The Glorp:** jelly aliens badly disguised as gym staff, in polos and tracksuits with name tags like "Hi! I'm GLRRK." Their speech is relentless sales positivity with something very wrong underneath.
- **The Membership Overlord (boss):** a Glorp queen in a **massage-chair mech** with a cup holder, a "shiatsu" stomp, and terms-of-service missiles.

## Levels (same shapes as the spec's beats; new dressing)

0. **Docking Bay: "Welcome, Valued Member!"**
   - Banners read "WHERE QUITTING IS NOT AN OPTION" and "New Year, New You (Forever)."
   - A cheerful hologram explains the cancellation policy. It is twelve pages long and Doc skips it.
   - Lockdown starts: "ATTENTION: A MEMBER IS ATTEMPTING TO LEAVE."
1. **Check-in Turnstiles** (was Kiosk Row; this is the current Gate C arena).
   - Cover is the front desk, the turnstiles, the supplement counter and a towel cart.
   - The turnstiles flash "ACCESS DENIED: MEMBERSHIP ACTIVE".
   - Four Reps behind the desk.
   - Teaching signs on the walls: "NO DROPPING WEIGHTS. NO DROPPING COVER." and "RE-RACK YOUR AMMO."
2. **Smoothie Bar** (was the Food Court).
   - Blenders and a "Build-Your-Bowl" counter.
   - The first Spawn Sauna opens and Personal Trainers pour out.
   - The **Free Trial** shotgun sits on the "Free Trial Counter" ("No commitment!*"; the asterisk is a commitment).
   - Reps flank from the yoga-studio mezzanine. One is mid-pose.
3. **Cardio Deck** (was the Escalators).
   - Rows of treadmills running at max speed give stepped cover up a ramp.
   - Spotters snipe from the spin-class balcony while an alien instructor yells "CLIMB!" over the music.
   - The **Calorie Counter** is at the top.
   - A screen on every treadmill shows Doc's heart rate.
4. **Free Weights and Locker Room** (was Bed and Bath).
   - Bench presses, dumbbell racks and squat racks.
   - Personal Trainers ambush disguised as "statues of our top members."
   - The Bulk arrives with Reps, and two Spawn Saunas open from the locker room steam.
5. **The Cancellation Desk**, the boss.
   - The top floor, in a velvet-roped room behind a "Please Take a Number" dispenser (Doc takes number 4,912,006; now serving: 3).
   - **Phase 1, Shiatsu Mode:** the Overlord's massage-chair mech stomps, kneads and fires terms-of-service missiles that unfurl as fine print.
   - **Phase 2, Retention Surge:** it retreats behind the desk and opens two Spawn Saunas ("Have you met our team?").
   - **Phase 3, The Final Offer:** desperate, it bargains between attacks: "One month free." "THREE months!" "SIX MONTHS AND A TOWEL!" Tobble: "Doc, that's actually a good—" Doc: "No."

## Ending

The Overlord falls. Doc reaches the desk, signs the form, and stamps it. The station's billing core sputters and every trapped member is freed.

A beat later, Doc's tablet chimes:

> *"We're sorry to see you go! Your cancellation is confirmed. A $49.99 cancellation fee has been applied. Come back anytime — 50% off your first month!"*

Doc stares. Tobble: "Ooh, fifty percent." Doc reloads The Regret. **Cut to credits.**

After the credits, a postcard arrives: "We miss you! — GlorpFit."

## Running gags

- **The shrinking hours.** Every checkpoint updates the cancellation hours: "Tues 2:00–2:15" becomes "2:00–2:10", then "2:00–2:05", then "By appointment", then "Cancellation Desk temporarily unstaffed."
- **The paperwork hunt.** Collectibles are cancellation forms:
  - "Form 27-B: Intent to Leave"
  - "Notarized Letter of Regret"
  - "Proof of Relocation (50+ miles)", where Doc asks "I live in orbit. Does that count?"
  - "Doctor's note"
  - "Exit Interview (mandatory, 90 minutes)"

  The last one is in the boss room.
- **The PA voice:**
  - "Reminder: re-racking weights is mandatory. Re-racking members is also mandatory."
  - "The pool is closed. The pool has always been closed."
  - "Members who attempt to leave will be enrolled in our Loyalty Program."
- **Tobble's tracker logs the fights:** "12 reps!" (kills), "Active minutes: 4," and "You closed your rings!"
- **Freed members** stumble out of tanning-bed pods:
  - "Is it still January?"
  - "I only came here for the hot tub. Once. In 2019."
  - "Do you know how to cancel a—" Doc: "Working on it."
