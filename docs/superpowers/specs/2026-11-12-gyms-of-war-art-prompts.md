# Gyms of War: prompts for an image generator

Paste the **context block** first, or at the top of each prompt, then one of the image prompts below it.

Many image tools refuse or water down prompts that name a living show or studio. The style is therefore described in plain terms. If your tool allows it, you can add "in the spirit of Rick and Morty" to the end of the context block.

## Context block (paste first)

> **Game:** "Gyms of War: No Pain, No Cancel" is a comedic third-person cover shooter, a loving parody of Gears of War.
>
> **Story:** Aliens called the Glorp have taken over GlorpFit Orbital, a giant gym on a space station, and they feed on members who never cancel. Our hero, Doc Vex, signed up on January 2nd seven years ago, went twice, and has been billed $49.99 every month since. Cancellation must be done in person, at the Cancellation Desk on the top floor. So Doc fights his way up the tower, floor by floor.
>
> **Art style:**
> - Cel-shaded adult-animation sci-fi cartoon look: thick, slightly wobbly black outlines; flat colour fills with one hard shadow tone; simple rounded shapes.
> - Characters have big expressive faces, droopy or bulging eyes and goofy proportions.
> - Pastel-meets-neon palette: lavender and purple walls, a cream and mint-teal checkerboard floor, hot-pink and magenta accents, a deep purple starfield with ringed planets visible through windows.
> - Alien goo is acid green, enemy shots are acid green, and the hero's gun and shots are bright orange.
> - Comedic and absurd, never gory: enemies burst into green jelly and paper receipts.
>
> **Rules:**
> - No real gym brands, logos or slogans.
> - No office or workplace setting. It's a sci-fi gym.

## Image prompts

### 1. Key art / box cover

> Hero shot for the box art. Doc Vex charges toward the viewer through a space-station gym in a heavy "roadie run" crouch, orange assault rifle in hand, with a tiny mint-green three-eyed alien intern scrambling behind him.
>
> Behind them are jelly-green aliens in purple staff polos, popping out from behind a front desk and turnstiles, and a huge banner reading "WHERE QUITTING IS NOT AN OPTION".
>
> Space and a ringed planet are visible through tall windows. Dynamic low angle, motion lines, cel-shaded, thick outlines, room at the top for a logo.

### 2. Gameplay mock screenshot

> An in-game screenshot from an over-the-shoulder third-person camera.
>
> - The hero crouches behind a waist-high kiosk counter (low cover) in the "Check-in Turnstiles" area of an alien-run space gym.
> - He leans out to aim an orange assault rifle at a green jelly alien in a purple polo and name tag, who is leaning out from behind a supplement counter with a glowing green blaster.
> - The HUD shows a small crosshair, ammo "50 / 250" in the bottom-right corner, and an "active reload" bar with a bright zone.
> - Fluorescent ceiling light strips run overhead, the floor is a pastel checkerboard, and the walls are lavender.
>
> Cel-shaded, thick outlines, readable and clean, as in a stylized 3D game.

### 3. Character sheet: Doc Vex (the hero)

> A character turnaround sheet (front, side, back, three-quarter) for Doc Vex, a buff, exhausted, deadpan scientist.
>
> - **Body:** a human, strong and broad-shouldered with thick arms and a slight V-taper, but still cartoony.
> - **Hair and face:** messy teal hair, permanent tired eye-bags, a flat unimpressed mouth.
> - **Outfit:** an off-white lab coat worn over a gym tank top, sweatband, chunky boots.
> - **Gear:** carries "The Regret", a bright-orange chunky assault rifle with a spinning pizza-cutter blade mounted under the barrel.
>
> Include 4 expression heads: bored, annoyed, determined, deadpan smirk. Cel-shaded, thick outlines, flat colours, plain background.

### 4. Character sheet: Tobble (the squadmate)

> A character turnaround and expression sheet for Tobble, a small, anxious, upbeat alien intern.
>
> - **Body:** about 0.7 times the hero's height, with stubby legs.
> - **Face and colour:** mint-green skin and three eyes.
> - **Gear:** a huge yellow backpack stuffed with snacks and a water bottle, a proudly worn "VISITOR" badge, a fitness tracker on one wrist, and a small blaster.
> - **Personality:** cheerful, nervous, easily tempted by deals.
>
> Include expressions: excited, panicked, "I got you! PUSH!" (cheering), tempted by a coupon. Cel-shaded, thick outlines, plain background.

### 5. Enemy sheet: the Glorp

> An enemy lineup sheet for the Glorp, translucent acid-green jelly aliens with two glowing yellow-green eyes, wearing badly fitting stolen gym-staff uniforms.
>
> 1. **"Rep":** a standard alien in a purple polo with a name tag reading "Hi! I'm GLRRK", holding a glowing green "upsell blaster", with a fake customer-service smile.
> 2. **"Personal Trainer":** a hulking alien in a tank top and sweatband, swinging a kettlebell, screaming motivation.
> 3. **"The Membership Overlord":** a giant boss, lilac and gold, wearing a crown and a lanyard heavy with keycards, smug and enormous.
>
> Show scale against a human silhouette. Cel-shaded, thick outlines, comedic, no gore, plain background.

### 6. Environment concept art: three floors

> Three environment thumbnails of an alien-run gym on a space station, all with ceilings, fluorescent light strips and space visible through windows:
>
> 1. **Check-in Turnstiles:** front desk, turnstile banks, supplement counter, a "FRONT DESK" sign, and a "CANCELLATIONS: TOP FLOOR" arrow.
> 2. **Smoothie Bar:** a blender bar, build-your-bowl counters, cafe tables, and a "FREE TRIAL — No commitment!*" sign.
> 3. **Cancellation Desk:** a velvet-roped boss room with a "PLEASE TAKE A NUMBER — NOW SERVING: 3" sign and steaming "spawn sauna" doors.
>
> Waist-high and tall props read clearly as cover. Pastel lavender, mint and cream, with magenta accents. Cel-shaded concept art.

### 7. Logo

> A video game logo: "GYMS OF WAR", with the smaller subtitle "NO PAIN, NO CANCEL" beneath.
>
> - **Lettering:** heavy, chiselled military-stencil block letters, slightly beveled and battle-worn, in the spirit of a gritty war-shooter logo, but made comedic.
> - **Emblem:** a cog or gear whose centre is a kettlebell (the kettlebell's handle forms the top of the gear), with a crack and a drip of acid-green alien goo.
> - **Colours:** gunmetal and off-white letters, with acid-lime green (#b6ff3c), orange and purple accents, on a dark background.
>
> Also show a square app-icon version with just the kettlebell-cog emblem. Clean vector look.

### 8. Weapons and props sheet

> A prop sheet:
>
> - "The Regret", an orange assault rifle with an under-barrel spinning pizza cutter called "Cheat Day";
> - "Guest Pass", a small orange pistol;
> - kettlebell grenades with a fizzing fuse;
> - a protein-tub ammo crate labelled "+25 scoops";
> - a steaming spawn-sauna door;
> - a supplement counter;
> - a turnstile.
>
> Cel-shaded, thick outlines, flat colours, with labels.

## Bringing images back

Save the images you like into the repo (for example `docs/art-ref/`) or paste them into the chat, and say which parts you like: proportions, faces, palette, the logo. The game builds its characters from code, so the most useful references are:

- character turnarounds (front and side);
- colour swatches;
- the logo as a flat image.

I can match proportions, colours and silhouettes closely. I can't trace exact painted details.
