# Gyms of War: prompts for an image generator

Paste the **context block** first, or at the top of each prompt, then one of the image prompts below it.

Many image tools refuse or water down prompts that name a living show or studio. The style is therefore described in plain terms. If your tool allows it, you can add "in the spirit of Rick and Morty" to the end of the context block.

## Context block (paste first)

> **Game:** "Gyms of War: No Pain, No Cancel" is a comedic third-person cover shooter, a loving parody of Gears of War.
>
> **Story:** Aliens called the Glorp have taken over GlorpFit Orbital, a giant gym on a space station, and they feed on members who never cancel. Our hero, Doc Vex, signed up on January 2nd seven years ago, went twice, and has been billed $49.99 every month since. Cancellation must be done in person, at the Cancellation Desk on the top floor. So Doc fights his way up the tower, floor by floor.
>
> **Art style:**
> - Cel-shaded adult-animation sci-fi cartoon look: thick, slightly wobbly black outlines; flat colour fills with one hard shadow tone; simple rounded shapes.
> - Characters have big expressive faces, droopy or bulging eyes and goofy proportions.
> - Pastel-meets-neon palette: lavender and purple walls, a cream and mint-teal checkerboard floor, hot-pink and magenta accents, a deep purple starfield with ringed planets visible through windows.
> - Alien goo is acid green, enemy shots are acid green, and the hero's gun and shots are bright orange.
> - Comedic and absurd, never gory: enemies burst into green jelly and paper receipts.
>
> **Rules:**
> - No real gym brands, logos or slogans.
> - No office or workplace setting. It's a sci-fi gym.

# Part 2: 2D sheets made for 3D generation (Meshy, Tripo and similar)

Concept art and image-to-3D input need different things. These prompts make reference sheets that 3D generators and auto-riggers handle well.

**The rules every sheet follows:**
- **A-pose:** arms out about 45°, legs slightly apart, hands open, fingers together.
- **No weapons or props in the hands.** Weapons get their own sheet.
- **Flat, even lighting:** no cast shadows, no rim light, no glow, no motion lines, no floating goo.
- **Plain white or very light grey background:** no floor grid, logo, text or labels.
- **Orthographic look:** no perspective, the camera at chest height. The front and side views line up: the same height, the head and feet at the same lines.
- **Simple flat colours** with one soft shadow tone. Outlines are fine. No fine painted texture or tiny details.

## Tips for the 3D tool

- Generate **one character per image**: crop to a single front view if the tool takes one image, or upload front, side and back if it supports multi-view.
- Pick the result whose **silhouette** matches best. Colours can be fixed later.
- Export **FBX** for Mixamo, and **GLB** too if offered.
- Mixamo auto-rigs humanoids: Doc and Tobble are fine. Blob aliens may need the tool's own auto-rigger (Tripo and Meshy both have one) or a manual rig.
