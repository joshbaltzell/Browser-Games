## Things in PLAN.md that are wrong or underspecified
1. **"Heat compass: a sensor on the toward/away actions that already exist" (M7).** `toward` moves to the richest food and `away` to the emptiest natives (sim.js 463 to 475). A heat compass needs new action ids (`hotter`, `cooler`) that reuse the same move arrays; redefining toward/away would change the old dishes.
2. **The 5 to 15% band does not fit a habitat where your cell lives in part of the water.** Measured: every intended fix on dish 12 sat under 5%. Habitat 2 uses 3 to 12%.
3. **Spatial transport is grid-dependent.** `popDiffuse` and moves are per grid step, so at 16x16 a cell crosses the dish 4 times faster than at 64x64 (in world 1 this hardly mattered). Habitat 2 scales the current and robot diffusion by `n / 16`, and even then 16x16 is optimistic near the floor: the forecast must run at 32x32 on these dishes. PLAN's "bars agree at 95%" is not reachable at 16x16 here.
4. **Sporulate is a loophole in a grazing dish** unless grazers eat spores (in the prototype, 80% of a carried colony hid as spores in the grazed zone). The spec lets grazers eat spores (real: larvae eat the mat whole) but not heat (real: spores survive heat).
5. **`baseState` is cached by grid size only** (sim.js 327). A temperature-shaped food map needs the dish in the key; PLAN's "bands from makeCap" misses this, and without it the settled natives would be world 1's.
6. **Heat-stable photosystem is a Power card, not Insides** (PLAN 3, image list): it is how the cell makes energy, and the real trade-off (worse in the cool) fits the Power slot.
