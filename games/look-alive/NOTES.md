# Look Alive: guided card prototype page (gameplay pass 2)

Open `index.html` (double-click works; no server, no modules, no fetch). Files: `index.html`, `style.css`, `draw.js` (canvas drawing and the cell diagram SVG), `render-gl.js` (the WebGL2 dish and the cell portrait), `game.js` (state, screens, wiring). `sim.js`, `cards.js`, `levels.js` carry the slot rules (RULES.md). Spec: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-16-look-alive/GAMEPLAY-V2.md`.

## Flow
Step bar (1 Briefing, 2 Watch, 3 Adapt, 4 Run, 5 Result) and one handler line are always on screen.
- Dish list: ten dishes in two groups (Learning the cell 1 to 5, Exams 6 to 10), problem line, cleared state (exam dishes show the best grade), the cell you start with. Dish N unlocks when N-1 is cleared (Unlock all button for testing).
- World briefing (once, before dish 1, and the World button in the top bar; shows `world-acidica.jpg`), then a short dish briefing with "your cell" as `card-factory.jpg` (the Factory unit) or a cell diagram of the carried build.
- Watch: when the carried cell fails, the Stats panel switches to the forecast bars for that cell with the failing one tagged, so the player arrives at Adapt knowing which bar to fix. The carried cell (`LookAliveLevels.carry(id)`, or your own saved win from the previous dish) runs unchanged at 4x and stops 12 ticks after the failure is plain (acid and exposed causes use the autopsy end tick, overgrew uses start+30, others the start tick). Skip jumps there. One-line verdict from `autopsy().text`, button "Adapt your cell". If the carried cell wins it says so and Continue goes to the result.
- Adapt: body-slot loadout (GAMEPLAY-V2 section 12; rules in RULES.md "Body slots"). Left: a large flat SVG of the cell (`LAD.cellArt` in `draw.js`), one group per slot: Power = glowing core, Envelope = two concentric rings (Layer 1 outer, Layer 2 inner), Insides = the fluid disc, Division = a pinch line across the middle, Signal = dots leaking at the upper right, Motor = a tail stub, Reflex = a sensor patch on the skin. Fitted cards change the drawing (pumps on the skin, thick Tight membrane, mesh Cell wall, hexagonal Sealed shell, amber Acid-proof fluid, Sync pulse hex seam, naps dotted line with z, copier double line, quorum dots, radio rings, green bio patch, cyan machine hexagon, amber fermentation core, hexagonal fuel cell). Each slot has an HTML label tag with a leader line ("Envelope: Proton pump + empty"); locked slots are dim with a lock and "Opens on dish N", or "Factory fitted. Opens on dish 4." when the Factory part is fitted; the slot that opened on this dish pulses (static under reduced motion) and the handler line points at it until the first change. Under the diagram: a Preview bar (a sentence from `LookAlive.diffWords`: "Fit Proton pump -> Acid defence: safe. Growth: a little slower. Energy 2 of 4."), the energy meter (Factory parts shown darker), and the Pace dial once it is open. Middle: options panel for the selected slot: cards with picture (`assets/cards/card-<id>.jpg`, a letter tile if missing), name, Biology or Machine (hexagon motif), then ONE row from `LookAlive.cardEffects().changes` (see the exam pass below): "Changes:" every stat it moves in plain words with 1 or 2 arrows, coloured by tone, then its energy. The Real line is behind the "Real biology" checkbox in the panel header (cards grow taller when it is on); the Game sentence is the card's tooltip. "Fitted" on the fitted one (its Helps and Costs are measured against the cell without it), the plain reason in a reserved line when an option cannot be fitted; an "Empty" row (not for Power); Envelope shows Layer 1 and Layer 2 rows; Division shows the Sloppiness dial as tuning; Reflex shows the bio cards and the Machine rule sentence builder. Hover or focus previews (preview sentence, ghost on the diagram, ghost markers and arrows on the bars); hovering or focusing a dial step previews it the same way; click fits, click the fitted option or Empty clears. Right: the **Forecast** (below), Tip, Undo, Reset to carried cell, RUN. Tags show new, swapped or removed against the carried cell. Tab reaches slot tags, options and dial steps, Enter fits.
- **Forecast (stat bars).** One row per stat in `dish.stats` (50 px, fixed): name, a reserved "this is what failed" tag slot, a reserved arrow slot, the track with the safe zone drawn as a green block, your marker (green when safe, red when not), a ghost marker (a dashed box, green or red by the state it would be in) and one verdict line in words (green or red). On preview the verdict line reads "-> Safe" or "-> Too slow: you will fade out" and the arrow shows the direction of the change, green when the stat gets safer or healthier, red when worse. If a preview would dissolve the colony, only the Acid bar is previewed (the other stats are meaningless then). After a failure the failing bar has a red border and the tag, a line under the bars says "Changes Acid defence: Envelope slot, Insides slot" (from `LookAlive.statFixers` on the build that failed) and those slots on the diagram and their tags are outlined amber ("can fix Acid defence"); once the stat is safe the line turns green ("... is safe now. Press Run."). The old acid line and the plain-words prediction list are gone (the bars say it); validate problems stay.
- Tip: ONE level, the second sentence of `LookAliveLevels.tip` (names one part or control). Free.
Everything fits 1280x720 without scrolling on dishes 1 to 3 (dish 4 envelope with four cards and dish 5 reflex scroll inside the options list).
- Run: replay at 2x. Live stats panel (the same bars, order and colours as the Forecast, read off the run at the current tick with `LookAlive.liveStats`; the dashed outline marker is the forecast for this build; Crash survival shows "Waiting for the crash" until the crash starts). The COVER gauge and the four needles are gone: the bars carry Smell and Naturalness, and COVER only restated them. The ribbon and the suspicion graph stay, with channels renamed Growth (speed), Smell, Growth (share), Naturalness. Immune boxes appear only for dishes with channels. Dish 1 shows "Colony alive: N%" and acid loss graphs. The dish event is marked on the graphs.
- Result: win gives stamp, cover name, notebook page, Next dish (the winning build is saved per dish and starts the next one). Loss gives the memo (Finding = the headline that names the stat, then a "Stat that failed" row) and a Tip button, then "Back to Adapt" keeps the build.

Pictures: the Watch and Run screens show the same figure at the top left of the dish; the replay's card strip has a small card thumbnail per card.

Keys: Space continues, runs, or plays/pauses; R restarts the watch or retries the run; Left/Right scrub 1 tick (Shift: 10); Escape closes the overlay. Progress, wins and world-seen are in `localStorage` (keys `bg.look-alive.*2`, all access in try/catch; saved builds go through `LookAlive.conform`).

## The dish view (2D fallback)
This is now only the fallback, used when WebGL2 is missing, the context is lost, frames stay slow at the lowest tier, or `__la.forceFallback(true)`. `LAD.drawCells`: fixed pools of candidate blobs (950 native, 1050 yours, 420 waste wisps) with fixed positions and thresholds. A blob shows when the density at its spot (interpolated between 10-tick snapshots) beats its threshold, so counts follow the population, positions are stable, and falling density shrinks then removes blobs. Acid loss also shrinks pink blobs. Green tint only while the immune stage is active. Slight deterministic drift; none under reduced motion.

## Dish renderer (render-gl.js, "dark field with depth and haze")
Ported from `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-16-look-alive/graphics-lab.html` (owner decisions 17 Nov in GRAPHICS-PLAN.md). Global `LookAliveRender`: `create(canvas, opts)` gives the dish (`setRun(run, dish, build, {slots, machine})`, `setTick`, `frame(ts)`, `rackFocus('watch'|'run'|'jump')`, `setQuality`, `resize`, `stats`, `signature(t)`, `destroy`), `portrait(canvas)` gives the Adapt cell picture. Plain script, shaders as strings, no fetch.

**Pipeline per frame:** haze/background shader (dish rim, waste haze, acid ambient, immune glow, fizz) -> additive cloud pass (tiny cells as soft blobs) -> instanced signed-distance cell pass (rim, interior, granules, tails, lattice, seams, membrane rings, pump dots, shell hexagon, dormant ovals, dissolve burn, engulf tint) -> specks (dust, food, acid bubbles, lens specks) -> half and quarter resolution bloom -> composite (vignette, edge fringing, grain, hue-preserving tone map). Depth gives each individual a blur radius (edge softness, bokeh disc when large), focal plane 0.5 = the robot band (robots depth 0.5 +- 0.05, natives spread +- 0.4, immune 0.62).

**Real data, stable identities.** `fieldsAt(t)` interpolates the 10-tick snapshots (native, robot, food, waste, Z+SP dormant) and rescales them by the per-tick history (`H.nat`, `H.rob` through a slow-release lag so acid losses read as a dissolve, `H.food`) so motion is smooth at any tick. Each of the 64x64 grid cells owns a fixed set of candidate individuals per species (random stream seeded by cell index, species and slot: position, threshold, size, depth, shape, flags). An individual is alive while `min(density/startNativeDensity, 1.3)/1.3` beats its threshold, and it grows or shrinks across a small band, so scrubbing, panning and replays show the same individuals and nothing pops. `signature(t)` hashes the packed instances (no jitter, no culling): tick 40 of dish 2 gives `51b76eea` before and after scrubbing to 150, 0, and zooming, and again on a fresh replay.
**Voids (visual only).** A fixed noise mask (colony blobs plus thin filaments, `maskAt`) thins which candidates exist for natives, with robots following it at 45% strength (`robotMask`). The mask is normalised by its mean over the disc, so each species' total count still follows its own density sum and the pink to violet ratio is unchanged by the mask. No simulation number is touched.
**Level of detail.** When more individuals would be on screen than `screenCells` for the tier (scaled by dish pixel area and zoom squared), a fixed subset by identity is drawn (`g4`). Natives are thinned by `thinN`, robots by `min(1, thinN * robotBoost)` (2.4), so zoomed out the pink share is overstated on purpose (you must stay findable). Zoom in and every individual is drawn at true size. Radius has a pixel floor (`minPxN` 1.4, `minPxR` 2.3) so cells stay legible at default zoom.

**Game moments.** Dissolve: stress from `H.acid` (burn pattern, shrink, bubbles, fizz in the haze, slow-release lag). Take over: robots follow density, natives thin. Crash: food specks follow the food field and vanish, cells dim up to 50% (`foodRatio` under 0.72). Dormant robots (Z and SP fields, plus 14% sleepers for Random naps) are small hard dim ovals. Exposed by Naturalness: seam strength = Regularity suspicion / 6 times (0.45 + 0.3 per Machine part), zero with no Machine parts; per-individual gating makes seams appear progressively, cells pulse in unison above 0.18. Immune: stage drives how many (9, 30, 70 at 20k, scaled by the square root of the tier) and how close (0.35, 0.72, 1.0 of the way from the rim to the colony), as smoothed per-tick series so they are stable under scrubbing; at stage 3 robots are tinted green and shrunk in proportion to `H.kill`.
**Immune motion (18 Nov).** The green cells are guards (white blood cells). Each enters from the rim, then patrols its own slow meandering route through the dish (two slow sinusoids per cell: `immRate` 0.16 rad/s, `immRoam` 0.35, deterministic in time, facing its heading). The stage pulls the route toward the colony (`immSpread` 0.6 of the old target scatter), where every cell circles it the same way (`immOrbit` 0.35 rad/s, `immRadius` 0.04). No motion with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
**Take-over density (18 Nov).** The on-screen cell cap grows with your share (`TUNE.crowd` 3.5: up to 4.5x the cells as share goes from 30% to 85%), so a take-over fills the dish with pink instead of looking sparse; natives stay thinned to a few.
**Living natives (18 Nov).** Natives and your cells drift slowly and identically (`TUNE.driftAmp` 0.03, about 2 px/s, plus a faint jiggle), so movement is no tell. A newborn grows out of its parent (the previous individual in the same grid cell, when alive) and settles at its own place; a dying one shrinks back toward its parent over `deathMargin` 0.03 of density instead of popping out. Deterministic in the tick and time, so the identity hash is unchanged by scrubbing.
**Build on the cell:** Envelope layers are membrane rings, Proton pump amber dots, Tight membrane thicker skin, Sealed shell a cyan hexagon, Acid-proof amber interior, Sync pulse a strong hex lattice, Head count three orbiting dots, a reflex a green (bio) or cyan hex (machine) skin patch, Power a core when large.

**Camera and focus.** Wheel and pinch zoom (1 to 16, around the cursor), drag to pan, double-click resets; the wheel handler calls `preventDefault`. Rack focus is a damped hunt (`rackFreq` 0.6, `rackDecay` 3, start -1.35, 2.0 s, blur boosted by `rackBoost` 1.6 and fading with the move) at exactly three moments: Watch begins, the player's Run begins (playback is held 1.1 s so the lens settles first), Jump to the moment / Skip to the trouble moment (gentle zoom to 2.1x at the densest robot block). Otherwise blur strength 1.0 (`TUNE.blur`, scaled by dish size) and a very slight focus breathing. `prefers-reduced-motion`: no rack focus, no hold, no breathing, no pulse, no drift (the jump zoom is applied instantly). Watch on dish 1 now defaults to 1x (the colony is gone in 12 ticks).

**Tiers and fallback.** Candidate pools: phone 5,000, laptop 20,000 (default; phone-like devices pick phone), high 50,000, from the Detail control in the dish corner (resolution scale 0.8 on phone, pixel cap 1.8 M). Frame time is watched; 50 slow frames (over 30 ms) step the tier down with a small note, and slow at phone tier falls back to flat dots. If WebGL2 is unavailable, shaders fail, or the context is lost, the GL canvas is dropped, the old 2D canvas `#runDish` draws with `LAD.drawCells`, and a small note says so. A canvas that has had a WebGL context cannot give a 2D one, so the page keeps two canvases. `__la.glStats()`, `__la.gl()`, `__la.glSig(t)`, `__la.forceFallback(bool)`, `__la.glPortrait()`.
**Adapt portrait.** `cellGL` canvas under the SVG, same shader. The SVG keeps the slot targets, highlights, leader lines, empty-slot outlines and the Division art; CSS (`.cellglon`) hides the SVG art of the fitted parts. Preview ghosts are merged into the slots before drawing. On failure the SVG art shows as before.
Natives (17 Nov): body fill darker in the middle with a bright rim, five internal granules and a soft nucleoid, per-individual bend and outline wobble (shape hash from the existing phase), all faded in by pixel radius (tiny path gets a filled body only). Identity hash untouched.
Tuning values live in `LookAliveRender.TUNE` (render-gl.js top).
**Look pass (17 Nov, after the take-over smear report).** The dish is only 320 css px, so the old 3,600-individual screen cap turned dish 2 into pink fog. Now: `screenCells` {phone 150, laptop 300, high 520} (about 300 distinct cells at zoom 1, scaling with zoom squared, so zoom 6 draws everything), pixel floors `minPxN` 3.1 / `minPxR` 3.7 (backing px times `eng.px`, so retina is not smaller), `cloud` 0.25 (additive cloud pass amplitude, was 1), `bloom` 0.35 with `bloomThr` 0.8 (was 1 and 0.6), `blur` 0.6 and `tol` 0.17 (fewer out-of-focus cells), waste haze at about half strength, robot colour gain 1.5. Pink share: `robotBoost` 1.7 now fades out between robot shares `boostLo` 0.06 and `boostUntil` 0.22, so dish 1 stays findable but from about 20% share the visible pink to violet ratio is the real one; a small note "Thinned view. Zoom in for every cell." (`.dthin`) shows at the bottom of the dish whenever the view is thinned and robots exist. Seams at zoom 1: `glint` 1.0 (a bright hexagon ring per seamed robot, gated per individual), `pulseGlow` 0.9 (unison brightness pulse), `pulseSize` 0.22 (unison size pulse), coarser lattice floor (2 px). The GL canvas is a circle (`border-radius: 50%` plus a CSS rim ring), the dish disc fills 95% of it (`baseScale` 0.475). Identity hash unchanged by scrubbing, zoom and replay (checked on dish 2 tick 40 after the pass: 696891fd stable across seek 150 + zoom 5 + seek 0).

## Habitat 3, "The thick water" (2026-11-20): dishes 15 and 16, every cell an individual
- **What it is.** A salt-flat habitat where scouts and natives are simulated one by one (`sim3.js`): run and tumble with a flagellum, slow tugs with pili, drift with gas vesicles, jitter with no motor. Food leaks from large green cells ("green boulders") that live and die, so food is in clumps. Dish 15 teaches that a cell that cannot move starves; dish 16 teaches "stop when fed" with a rule line (WHEN, DO, OTHERWISE) and a new suspicion channel, Movement, that compares how much the colony moves with how much the natives do. Plan and approved scope: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-20-look-alive-habitat-3/PLAN.md` (section 0).
- **How it is wired.** A dish with `agents` is handed to `sim3.js` from guarded branches in `sim.js`; `gameRun` shapes the result like any other run (histories, binned snapshots) and keeps the recording (`frames`, `foods`, `boulders`, `deaths`). The stat bars on these dishes are computed from the real run (cached per build), so a bar cannot read Safe on a build that loses. `render-gl.js` draws each recorded individual (`packAgents`): squares, rods and curved rods for the three natives, pink scouts, green boulders, an amber haze for food. The simple view uses `draw3.js`. 1x playback is 8 ticks a second on these dishes. Movement is at real scale; growth is about a hundred times faster than real.
- **Dev scripts.** `measure-h3.js` (the sim's own lessons over 10 seeds), `smoke-h3.js` (the game API on dishes 15 and 16), `pixel-h3.js` (drawn cells match the recording), `walkthrough-h3.js` (tab, intro, both dishes by real clicks, reload, compare, fallback), `dev/h3-lab/` (a tuning page with sliders and "Copy settings").
